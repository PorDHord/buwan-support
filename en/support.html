Where is my work stored?

Buwan 1.0 stores fabrics, drafts, and works on the current iPhone. It does not sync them through iCloud. Deleting the app can delete its local data.

How do I keep a copy of my work?

Use “Export My Buwan Data” and store the exported copy somewhere you control. Version 1.0 cannot import or restore it. The file is for your own records or a future version that explicitly supports import; future support is not promised. It is not a complete backup, device-transfer, or disaster-recovery solution.

How do I restore Buwan Pro?

Open Buwan Pro and choose “Restore Purchases.” Purchases use the Apple Account and App Store services available on the device.

What should I include in a support request?

Include the Buwan version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce the issue. Do not attach private photos or exported data unless they are necessary and you intentionally choose to share them.
