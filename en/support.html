Where is my work stored?

Buwan stores fabrics, drafts, and works on the current iPhone. It does not sync them through iCloud. Deleting the app can delete its local data.

Backup and restore in version 2.0

Version 2.0 can export and restore fabrics, works, and patchwork drafts. When restoring, choose to merge with or replace local data. A copy of the current data is retained before applying the restore. Keep an exported backup somewhere you control before uninstalling the app. Automatic cloud sync is not included. Version 1.0 supports export only.

How do I restore Buwan Pro?

Open Buwan Pro and choose “Restore Purchases.” Purchases use the Apple Account and App Store services available on the device.

What should I include in a support request?

Include the Buwan version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce the issue. Do not attach private photos or exported data unless they are necessary and you intentionally choose to share them.
