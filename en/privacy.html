Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-10

Buwan helps you preview fabric combinations before sewing. Buwan stores your content on this device: it does not sync your fabrics, drafts, works, or photos through iCloud or a Buwan-operated server.

Information used by the app

You may choose or photograph fabric, design patterns, and save fabrics, drafts, and works. Image processing and storage happen on your device. Buwan does not create a separate account and contains no advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDK.

Photos and camera

Buwan requests access only after you choose the corresponding action: selecting a fabric photo, taking a fabric photo, or saving an exported preview to Photos. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Apple is the third-party service provider that processes Buwan Pro purchases and purchase restoration through StoreKit for that purpose. The publisher does not receive your payment-card details. Apple’s terms, privacy policy, and security obligations apply to Apple’s processing.

Data export and deletion

In version 2.0, you can export and restore backups of fabrics, finished-product works, free-patchwork works, and drafts. Restore offers a choice to merge or replace local data, and prepares a recovery copy before applying changes. Version 1.0 supports export only. Copies you export or share are controlled by the destination you choose; Buwan does not automatically upload them. Local content remains until you delete it in Buwan, replace it during restore, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app.

Support communications

If you email support, the publisher and the email service provider used by the publisher process the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send only to respond and diagnose the reported issue, subject to applicable privacy and security obligations. Support messages are retained only as long as needed to reply, diagnose the issue, and meet applicable legal requirements. You may request deletion through the same support address, except for information that must be retained by law. Do not send private fabric photos unless they are necessary and you intentionally choose to share them.

Security and changes

Buwan uses the iOS app sandbox and integrity checks to reduce accidental corruption, but no storage system can guarantee absolute security. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
